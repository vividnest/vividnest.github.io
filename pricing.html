Local currency pricing

Paddle automatically shows localized prices at checkout based on your location, currency, and applicable tax. The prices above are shown in USD; the checkout will display the exact currency, taxes, trial terms, and renewal amount before payment.

Subscription terms: Monthly, 6-month, and yearly plans are recurring subscriptions. A payment method is required to start the 7-day free trial (trial applies to the monthly plan; check checkout for trial eligibility on other plans). If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the selected subscription renews at the displayed recurring price. The Lifetime plan is a one-time payment, not a subscription. Refunds and transaction support for purchases processed through Paddle are handled through Paddle.
